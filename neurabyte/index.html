The method underneath

The AI Council.

What runs beneath every tool on this page: specialised models analyse the same question independently, disagreement is surfaced rather than averaged away, and the final judgment stays human. The architecture is public. The implementations that use it are not.

The through-line of this arm, stated plainly: every tool here assumes the machine will be confidently wrong at some point, and that the whole value of a trained mind is being the one who notices. That is why the prompt kit ships with judgment attached, and why AI Playground is addressed to children rather than to professionals — the habit has to be built before it is needed.
