FAQ

The questions that matter

Who owns the data and the accounts?
You do. You remain the controller of your data and the owner of every tool account the agents use. We operate inside access you grant and can revoke.
What if the agent makes a mistake?
Agents never take irreversible actions on their own — sending, deleting, paying, publishing all require human approval, yours or ours, depending on what we agree per workflow. And because we monitor the data itself, failures surface in our alerts, not in your client's inbox.
Do you build agents that make decisions about people?
No. We don't build automation for hiring, credit, insurance or health decisions about individuals. That's a hard line, for your legal safety and ours.
Why a subscription instead of a one-off build?
Because a one-off build starts dying the day it's delivered — APIs change, rate limits change, formats change. What you're paying for monthly is that someone is watching, fixing and improving it. That's also why we host: without telemetry, nobody sees the failure until your client does.
How do we start?
Message us on Telegram. Our assistant collects what the task is, how often it happens and which tools are involved — then a human comes back to you with scope and price. Usually the same day. The assistant is itself one of our agents: you'll be testing the product before you pay for it.
