Non-negotiable principles

Built so trust is structural, not verbal.

Six principles govern everything SYNEX does — in code, in contract, in the book. They are not marketing lines; they are enforced by design.

🔐

No custody

Your capital stays in your exchange account. SYNEX operates through an API with minimal permissions — read and trade only. Never withdrawal. Never asset transfer.

🛡️

Minimal permissions

Keys are accepted with Read+Trade only. A key with withdrawal or transfer permissions is automatically blocked (Security Layer 3) until corrected.

📒

Auditable book from day one

Everything that happens from the moment of signature is written to your individual audit book — append-only, timestamped, hash-chained. Who did what, when. Proven.

📊

Projected, never promised

Profits are projections built on 100% empirical calculation — registered production, validated backtests, walk-forward. Never a guarantee. The journey is documented: success, partial or failure.

🧭

Directed communication

You receive what matters, when it matters. Self-service is unlimited and free; assisted support runs on a monthly allowance — wait, never a surprise charge. SLA: senior analyst within 24 business hours.

⚖️

Founder control, forever

The Founder's golden share is never diluted. Investor participation is economic — never voting, never governance. Caps: ≤1% per client, ≤5% aggregate.

The engine

One engine. Both directions.

SYNEX operates long and short — developed symmetrically so it can generate results in any market regime: bull, bear or sideways. Your capital does not bet on the market's luck. It bets on the robustness of the engineering.

LONG LEG

Buys strength, exits on exhaustion

The long leg enters confirmed strength and exits on exhaustion — Keltner, RSI extremes, break-even protection. Developed with the same rigour as the short leg.

SHORT LEG

Sells weakness, covers on capitulation

The short leg works the fall — same symmetry, same discipline. If one direction fails, the other compensates: directional redundancy.

Security — 7 layers

Defence is layered, and layers are enforced.

1 · Minimal permissions

Read + Trade. Never withdrawal. Never asset transfer — the critical point.

2 · Dedicated sub-account

The strategy operates exclusively in your "SYNEX-TRADING" sub-account. Your main account is untouchable.

3 · Automatic block

Key with wrong permissions → account blocked on the platform until corrected. Blocking is proof of diligence.

4 · Automated movement

Spot↔futures moves inside the sub-account only — logged, minimal, contract-authorized.

5 · Individual audit

Per-client Obsidian book — append-only + hash chain. Detects and records any manual action that diverges from the strategy.

6 · Contract + legal preservation

Express authorization, clear responsibilities, auto-block as evidence of diligence.

7 · AI support that guards

Guides you to never enable transfer, watches conversations, escalates emergencies to the Founder.
