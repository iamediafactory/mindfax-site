I · The finding

Markets contain a mechanical habit. We measured it.

Not a pattern someone noticed on a chart. A structural property of how modern electronic markets are built, present across instruments and across years, and measurable independently of any view on direction.

Abstract · non-technicalMethod withheld

A recurring structural inefficiency exists in modern electronic markets. It can be identified without forecasting, it survives out-of-sample testing, and it is exploitable under strict risk discipline — not as a certainty, but as an edge measurable over a sufficient number of events.

Evidence base
Official exchange candles across multiple instruments and multiple years, with real funding and modelled execution cost — never synthetic data.
Out-of-sample
Strict walk-forward: parameters fixed on one period, measured on another that was never seen during design.
Robustness
Sensitivity mapped across a configuration grid. We require a plateau, not a peak — a lone optimum is treated as overfitting, not as a discovery.
Ruin analysis
Monte Carlo path simulation, used to map the distribution of drawdown paths and to bind position size; treated as a stress map, not as proof that ruin is impossible.
The conditions themselves, their thresholds and the way a position earns the right to grow are not published here. A venture investor receives them under agreement, in the separate equity track. This is the only asset in the company that cannot be rebuilt once it is given away.

III · Falsification

What would prove us wrong.

A claim that cannot fail is not a finding, it is a sales pitch. These are the conditions under which we would conclude that we are wrong — written down in advance, so that they cannot be renegotiated later.

01

The forward record diverges materially from what the out-of-sample study projected, over a number of events large enough to be meaningful rather than unlucky.

02

The advantage proves to depend on one configuration rather than a broad region — the plateau collapses into a peak.

03

Real execution cost, slippage and funding consume the edge in live conditions, having been survivable in modelling.

04

The structural habit itself decays as market microstructure changes — the inefficiency is arbitraged away by others.

Each of these is monitored continuously, and each is reported to founding users whether or not it is convenient. A venture is not a promise that nothing goes wrong; it is an agreement about what happens when it does.

A founding user of a build — not a client of a promise.

You are licensing a trading system at its earliest stage. The terms are written to keep that honest: your capital stays in your own account, in full, and nothing is transferred to us at any point.

Your capital
100% remains in your own account, in your own name, at all times. No funds are transferred to us — ever. We do not custody, deposit, withdraw or hold title to anything.
What you pay for
A founding-user licence to the software. It is a licence fee — not a contribution, not a subscription of capital — and it is never repaid as a function of results.
What a founding user receives
Licence price frozen for life, each version ahead of general release, a direct line to the builder, a voice in what gets built next, and a numbered place in the founding record.
The key
Minimum scope, in an account that remains yours. A key carrying withdrawal rights is refused by the software — and the refusal is written into your book.
Control
You may revoke the key at the exchange at any second, without notice or reason. That single act ends all access, independently of us.
Risk
Venture stage, experimental software. Capital can be lost in full. Obligation of means, not of result. Reported monthly as projection against realised, with the data — not with excuses.
