The laboratory

Where the theory is actually being tested.

Informal startup · in testing

Symbiosis Fractal Capital

Finsage argues that a system should hold the discipline a human cannot. SFC is where that argument is being put under load: a human-governed autonomous protocol operating on live market and exchange APIs, built so that no part of it can quietly decide on its own.

  • An AI council. Specialised models analyse the same decision independently; divergence is surfaced rather than averaged away; the human makes the final call.
  • Separation of powers. Decision, execution and reporting are architecturally distinct. Credential scope and fund-movement limits are explicit; canonical risk parameters cannot be changed silently.
  • Ghost Trade. A virtual-execution protocol that tracks decisions as if they were real, so a strategy is validated against outcomes before capital is exposed.
  • Evidence discipline. A readiness review scoring 45/100 and a documented operating failure are kept in the record rather than removed. AI-generated performance figures that failed internal audit were discarded rather than published.

What is not on this page, deliberately: strategy parameters, positions, returns. No performance is claimed here and none should be inferred. The architecture is disclosable; the edge is not — and unverified numbers are worse than no numbers.
